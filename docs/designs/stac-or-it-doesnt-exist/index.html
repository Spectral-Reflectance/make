STAC or It Doesn't Exist

An unofficial bag tag celebrating open geospatial data and the SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog specification. This downloadable version has no NFC chip.

Customise this design →

Download the files

Choose White, Blue & Gold; Gold, Purple & Orange; White, Maroon Red & Yellow; or Blue, Gold & White. You can also set your own three colours in the customiser. The base is 60 × 100 × 2 mm, with raised artwork on the front.

  • Download White, Blue & Gold 3MF
  • Download Gold, Purple & Orange 3MF
  • Download White, Maroon Red & Yellow 3MF
  • Download Blue, Gold & White 3MF
  • Download single-colour STL

Before printing

The 3MF includes separate parts for each colour. Open it in Bambu Studio, assign your filaments, and slice the model.

The STL version doesn't include colour information.

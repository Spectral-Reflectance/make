Trust the Agent, Blame the Human

An Earth Observation and AI-themed bag tag first made for the Agentic AI for EO workshop in Berlin. This downloadable version has no NFC chip.

Download the files

Choose White, Maroon & Yellow or White, Blue & Gold, or set your own three colours in the customiser. The base is 60 × 100 × 2 mm, with raised artwork on the front.

  • Download White, Maroon & Yellow 3MF
  • Download White, Blue & Gold 3MF
  • Download single-colour STL

Before printing

The 3MF includes separate parts for each colour. Open it in Bambu Studio, assign your filaments, and slice the model.

The STL version doesn't include colour information.
